Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how Moving Parts ("we") handles information in connection with our website (https://movingpartexplained.github.io) and Moving Parts Publisher, the private tool we use to publish our own videos.

1. Who this applies to

Moving Parts Publisher is used only by the owner of the Moving Parts channel to manage the Moving Parts accounts. It is not available to the public, and it never accesses accounts belonging to anyone else.

2. YouTube API Services

Moving Parts Publisher uses the YouTube API Services to upload our videos and manage their details and playlists. By using Moving Parts Publisher, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information accessed through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google, TikTok or Instagram (Meta), Moving Parts Publisher receives an access token that lets it act on that owner's own account only. It uses this to:

  • upload videos and set titles, descriptions, tags, categories, privacy and schedule;
  • create playlists on the owner's channel and add videos to them;
  • read basic channel or account details (such as the channel name) to confirm the correct account is connected;
  • record the ID and link of each published post so a video is never posted twice.

Moving Parts Publisher does not access, collect or store information about viewers or any other users, and it does not use YouTube API data for advertising or analytics about other people.

4. How information is stored

Access tokens and post records are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server we operate, sold, or shared with anyone, except that requests are sent directly to Google (YouTube), TikTok and Meta (Instagram) to perform the actions above. Those companies handle data under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy, TikTok Privacy Policy, Instagram Privacy Policy.

5. Cookies and this website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising on it. GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) to operate and secure its service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google / YouTube: revoke Moving Parts Publisher's access at any time from the Google security settings page.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites.

Revoking access stops Moving Parts Publisher immediately. Stored tokens and post records are deleted from the owner's computer when access is revoked or within 30 days of a request. To ask about or request deletion of any data, email movingparts.channel@gmail.com.

7. Children

Moving Parts Publisher is not directed at children and collects no information from them.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.

9. Contact
