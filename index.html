About Moving Parts

Moving Parts makes clear, friendly animations that show how the things around us actually work, starting with How Roads Work: roundabouts, intersections, highways and the rules that keep traffic moving. New Shorts are published on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok as @movingpartexplained.

Moving Parts Publisher

Moving Parts Publisher is a private tool that Moving Parts uses to publish its own videos. It runs on the creator's own computer and, after the channel owner signs in, uploads finished videos to the Moving Parts accounts on YouTube (via the YouTube API Services), Instagram and TikTok, and sets each post's title, description, tags, schedule and playlist. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts or data. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
